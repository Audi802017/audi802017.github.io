About

I am a tenured Associate Professor of Economics in the IO Group at KU Leuven, where I teach Microeconomics and supervise PhD and master students. I am also affiliated with Sciences Po and CEPR. My research agenda involves understanding individual and firm incentives in (bidding) markets. Ultimately, with the aim to improve the organization of these markets and to advance public policy.
